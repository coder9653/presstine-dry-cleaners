About Us

Boca Raton's Most Trusted Dry Cleaners

Presstine Dry Cleaners is a family-owned, eco-friendly dry cleaning service based in Boca Raton, Florida. Formerly known as Addison Court Cleaners, our team brings over 55 years of combined experience in professional garment care.

We use only PERC-free, non-toxic cleaning methods that are safer for your clothes, your family, and the environment. Our owner, is a certified environmental dry cleaner who personally oversees quality on every order.

Whether you need everyday dry cleaning, wedding gown preservation, or care for designer garments, we deliver exceptional results with free pickup and delivery across Boca Raton and Delray Beach.
