About Presstine Dry Cleaners

Boca Raton's trusted family-owned dry cleaner with 55+ years of combined experience in professional garment care.

Call (561) 999-0043

Presstine Dry Cleaners is a family-owned, eco-friendly dry cleaning service based in Boca Raton, Florida. Formerly known as Addison Court Cleaners, our team brings over 55 years of combined dry cleaning experience to serve our Palm Beach County community.

Our owner, John Baleno, is a certified environmental dry cleaner with extensive credentials including OSHA certification, comprehensive spotting certification, and textiles and fibers certification. He personally oversees quality on every order and can train hands-on in all phases of pressing and dry cleaning.

We are committed to using only PERC-free, non-toxic cleaning methods that are safer for your clothes, your family, and the environment. Our eco-friendly processes deliver the same professional-grade clean without the harsh chemicals, leaving your garments softer and fresher.
